What UrbanResolve Does

Turns scattered complaints into accountable civic action.

01

Report with evidence

Residents can pin the exact location, upload images, add videos with audio, and use speech-to-text for accessibility.

02

Detect common problems

Nearby similar complaints are grouped into hotspots so admins can identify repeated potholes, garbage zones, drainage issues, and water leaks.

03

Prioritize fairly

Severity scoring considers category, community support, issue age, and locality density to bring urgent reports to the top.

04

Verify the fix

Authorities upload on-site resolution videos, and residents can confirm whether the problem was actually solved with image proof.

How It Works

Simple for citizens. Useful for administrators.

Report

Submit the issue with location, media, and contact email.

Cluster

The platform surfaces duplicate complaints and severity priority.

Resolve

Admins update progress, attach proof, and notify affected users.

Verify

Nearby residents confirm whether the resolution is genuine.
